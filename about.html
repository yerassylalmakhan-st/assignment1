About Fantasy Premier League

Fantasy Premier League is the official fantasy football game of the English Premier League, drawing over 10 million players globally each season. Managers compete for rank, bragging rights, and mini-league titles across 38 gameweeks.

Key Historical Milestones

  1. 1991: Early fantasy football games launch in UK print newspapers.
  2. 2002: The official Premier League website launches its free online game.
  3. 2014: Mobile app integration boosts weekly active transfers worldwide.
  4. 2026: Over 11 million global active users compete in mini-leagues.

Essential Rules & Facts

  • Maximum of 3 players allowed from any single Premier League team.
  • Transfers cost 4 points each if you exceed your weekly free limit.
  • Captains earn double points; Vice-Captains step in if your Captain doesn't play.
  • Wildcard chips allow unlimited free squad changes twice per season.
