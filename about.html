About Fantasy Premier League

Learn the core mechanics, squad rules, and tactical chips that power Fantasy Premier League.

Squad Building Rules

Managers are given a budget of £100.0m to assemble a squad of 15 Premier League players:

  • 2 Goalkeepers
  • 5 Defenders
  • 5 Midfielders
  • 3 Forwards

Scoring Points

Players earn points based on their real-world actions during each gameweek:

  • Goals: Forwards (4 pts), Midfielders (5 pts), Defenders (6 pts).
  • Assists: 3 points for every goal setup.
  • Clean Sheets: 4 points for Goalkeepers and Defenders.
