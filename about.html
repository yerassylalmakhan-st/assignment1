About Fantasy Premier League

Fantasy Premier League is a game of strategy where you act as a real-world manager with a £100.0m budget.

Core Rules

  • Select a 15-player squad (2 Goalkeepers, 5 Defenders, 5 Midfielders, 3 Forwards).
  • Earn points each gameweek based on real-life goals, assists, clean sheets, and saves.
  • Make 1 free transfer per week to adapt to player injuries and upcoming fixtures.
