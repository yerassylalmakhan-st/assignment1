Welcome to Fantasy Football Hub

What is Fantasy Premier League?

Fantasy Football lets you act as a real manager, building a squad of Premier League players who earn points based on their actual real-world match performances each gameweek.

  • Select 15 players within a £100.0m budget limit
  • Earn points for goals, assists, clean sheets, and bonus points
  • Compete in mini-leagues against your friends and global managers

How to Get Started

Managing your squad efficiently requires strategic planning, monitoring player form, and watching fixture schedules.

  1. Pick your starting 11 and choose a Captain to score double points.
  2. Use your weekly free transfer to swap underperforming players.
  3. Play chips like Wildcard, Triple Captain, or Bench Boost at key moments.
